Both published notes retain `작성자 검토 전`. The five author-supplied figures replace their matching placeholders, two in 5.7.1 and three in 5.7.2, with unchanged image bytes.

The interactive belongs at the supplied Interactive 5.7-1 position in the second manuscript. Insert `[demo:pixelcnn-blind-spot]` on its own line in a Note or Article. Standalone: `demos/pixelcnn-blind-spot.html`. It uses the existing lazy iframe and resize mechanism and is packed into the portable preview.

This is the manuscript's spatial, grayscale 3×3 example, not the full original 7×7-first-layer RGB PixelCNN. Layer 1 uses Mask A, later layers use Mask B. Source: [van den Oord et al. (2016), masking and PixelCNN sections](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/oord16.pdf).

Reachability is computed by traversing the actual unrolled mask graph, with zero padding excluded. Blind spots are past pixels absent even from the saturated graph. Other unreachable past pixels are marked Beyond depth. No learned probabilities, synthetic images or tutorial panels are displayed.

Validation: independent forward impulse convolutions agree with graph traversal for every 5×5 target and depths 1–8. Future/current inputs never appear. For q, the first layer reaches k,l,m,p; j,n,o stay blind and other past inputs become reachable by depth 3. Browser QA covers corner targets, slider/keyboard/reset, mobile layout, note math, lazy loading and parent-frame resizing. The two manuscript bodies are unchanged apart from replacing the figure and interactive placeholders. All five images were supplied by the author; no external source or license is asserted.
